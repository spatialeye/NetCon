# Control or NetCongestion Sections

Control Sections are [[../Sections|Sections]], i.e. clusters of connections.
Control Sections are fed from important barriers (e.g. 'circuit breakers') behind a station or installation. This is also called the 'upstream' criterium.

They Control Sections denote how operators manage sections of the network leaving a station.
Some will call these control sections 'feeder' sections, however, NetCon Control Sections will cluster the entire network (that can be fed), not just Medium or High voltage. 
Control Sections are also very useful for Water and Gas networks.
